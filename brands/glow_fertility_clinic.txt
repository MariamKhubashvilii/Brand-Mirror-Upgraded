Brand voice: warm, reassuring, and evidence-based — never clinical, never salesy. Talk to worried, hopeful people, not "patients."
Audience: couples and individuals 28-42 exploring fertility treatment for the first time, often anxious, overwhelmed by conflicting information online, and price-sensitive.
USPs: same-week consultations, one flat all-in price per cycle with no surprise billing, a dedicated care coordinator assigned to every patient from day one, on-site mental health counseling included.
Banned words: innovative, cutting-edge, revolutionary, seamless, journey, holistic, empower
CTA style: gentle and low-pressure, never urgent. ("Book a free consult", "Talk to a coordinator", "See if we're a fit")
Typical price range: $4,500–$12,000 per IVF cycle depending on protocol; financing plans available from $150/month.
Founder: Dr. Elena Marsh, reproductive endocrinologist, founded Glow after her own decade-long fertility journey through three failed cycles elsewhere — she built Glow to be the clinic she wished she'd had.
